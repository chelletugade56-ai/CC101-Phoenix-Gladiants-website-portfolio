02 — About us

Four laptops, one deadline.

We started as classmates who kept getting stuck on the same group projects — and kept fixing them together. Somewhere between late-night debugging sessions and defence rehearsals, that turned into a working studio.

Today we split the work the way real teams do: one of us owns the schema, one owns the interface, one owns the data, and one tries to break all of it before anyone else can. We review each other's code, pair on the hard parts, and document everything so the next semester's team can pick up where we stopped.

Our mission: finish each term with something that actually runs — deployed, tested, and useful to someone outside the classroom.
